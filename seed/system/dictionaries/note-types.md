# Note types. Changed by the types right. A type with its own fields has a file: note-types/<type>.yaml.
thought — the owner's insight or idea on a topic, not tied to a day or period (a look back at a period → reflection). Test: still true when reread a year later, with no date
reflection — the owner looking back at a day or a period of the owner's life (a journal entry, a year or sprint review): what happened, how it felt, lessons. Test: without its date the note loses its sense. A strong conclusion worth keeping on its own → also a separate thought that links to the entry
idea — something the owner might do or make
decision — a choice the owner made, with why
preference — what the owner likes and how
owner-data — facts about the owner, and the owner's links, accounts and numbers that serve no project; the note says which kind. How the owner lived an episode, with feelings and lessons → reflection
fact — a fact about the world that the owner keeps
saved — outside material kept for later: a post, video, message or article, or a summary of one
book — an outside book kept as a resource
tool — an outside tool, app or service the owner may use
place — a place in the world the owner may go to
template — text to reuse, prompts too
record — what happened at a call or meeting on no project
list — many of one kind in one note
person — context about someone
hub — a topic page that links notes on one subject
